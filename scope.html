Workflow Sprint — scope and terms

A fixed-scope engagement for one business workflow that is missing, fragile or failing silently: built or repaired in the customer's own accounts, verified against acceptance criteria agreed in writing, and handed over with a runbook. This page sets out what is included, how acceptance is decided, the price and what is excluded.

Scope

  • One workflow in n8n, Make or Zapier, built new or repaired
  • At most three connected systems
  • One agreed trigger, one defined output and one error-alert path

Start and delivery

The delivery clock starts once four inputs are in place: the signed scope, complete least-privilege access, representative test data and a named approver. The workflow is tested and handed over within 72 business hours of that point. Client-side delays, platform outages and unavailable third-party APIs pause the clock.

Deliverables

  • The working workflow, in customer-owned accounts
  • An error alert to an agreed email address or team channel
  • A recorded acceptance test using the agreed test case
  • A short runbook covering the trigger, field mapping, credentials, error path and safe rerun steps
  • Seven calendar days of fixes for defects against the agreed acceptance test

Before you hire: the Failure Map

Before any contract, Pakvaro can send a free one-page Failure Map of your workflow: where it can fail without anyone noticing, and the exact test that would prove each point fixed. It is a diagnosis based on what you share publicly or in your brief; it never needs passwords or account access.

Acceptance

Before work starts, the test input, the expected result, validation and duplicate behaviour, a forced-failure check and the evidence to be recorded are written down and agreed. Acceptance occurs when the recorded test produces the agreed result, the error alert reaches the agreed destination and the runbook is delivered.

A new system, field, branch or business rule is a scope change handled through written change control, not a defect.

Price and payment

Founding price for the first three clients: USD 249 / INR 12,500.
Standard price thereafter: USD 499 / INR 25,000.

International: a fixed-price milestone funded into Upwork escrow before work starts. Pakvaro submits it together with a recording of the agreed acceptance test, and you release it once you have watched that test pass. (Upwork releases a submitted milestone automatically after 14 days if no action is taken.) Direct clients contract through Upwork Direct Contracts, so their payment is held in escrow too. If a Direct Contract is not possible, 50% to start and 50% after the recorded acceptance test.

Optional first step: USD 49 for a signed acceptance test, an access check and a fixed quote, credited in full against the Sprint.

India: 50% to start and 50% after the recorded acceptance test. No GST is charged while Pakvaro is not GST-registered.

Third-party subscriptions, platform usage and API fees are not included in the price; they are paid by the customer directly to those providers.

Exclusions

The following are outside the Workflow Sprint:

  • Production data migrations
  • Regulated, medical or financial decisions
  • 24/7 support
  • Unlimited changes
  • Scraping private data
  • A fourth system
  • Additional workflows
  • New branches, fields or rules not recorded in the scope
  • Unavailable or unsupported third-party APIs
  • Platform or vendor outages

Cure and defect fixes

If a Pakvaro-caused, in-scope acceptance check fails, Pakvaro receives one cure window of up to five business days. If the same check still fails after that cure, the customer may cancel and receive the full milestone back.

The seven-calendar-day defect-fix period begins on acceptance. It covers reproducible non-conformance with the agreed test — not additions or third-party changes.

Customer responsibilities

The customer owns and pays for all accounts, subscriptions and API usage; provides lawful authority and representative redacted or test data; maintains backups; names an approver; and removes Pakvaro's access after handover.

Credentials, keys, production exports and confidential code are never sent through ordinary email or entered into consumer tools.

Business days and hours are those defined in the signed statement of work, which governs each engagement and prevails over this page.

Partner delivery

The same scope and price apply when invoiced to an agency partner. The partner remains the commercial lead and confirms written end-client authority before any access. Deliverables may carry the partner’s brand. Non-solicitation covers only end clients introduced in writing, for 12 months.
