The engineering standard

Every workflow is built to be verified, not just delivered.

Most automation failures are silent: a lead lost between tools, a duplicate record, an expired connection nobody notices. Every Pakvaro build is engineered against those failure modes.

01

Acceptance criteria first

The expected result, test inputs and evidence are agreed in writing before the build begins, so the finish line is visible from day one.

02

Failure handling as standard

Validation, de-duplication, retries and error alerting are part of every workflow. Failures surface to a person instead of disappearing.

03

A handover your team owns

Everything is built in your accounts, with a runbook and a recorded walkthrough, so your team can operate, audit and extend it.

Approach

A short, transparent path from brief to working system.

  1. 01

    Scope the workflow

    You share the goal, the systems involved and a redacted example. Scope, acceptance criteria and evidence are agreed in writing. Please do not email passwords, keys or production exports.

  2. 02

    Build and verify

    The workflow is built in your accounts and run against the agreed tests, including the failure paths, not just the happy path.

  3. 03

    Review it working

    Before the final payment, you receive a short video of your workflow running in a test setup, so you approve what you have seen.

  4. 04

    Accept and hand over

    The recorded acceptance test is run, the runbook is delivered, and a defect-fix period begins on acceptance.

Engagements

Start with one workflow.

A fixed-scope Workflow Sprint for one workflow that is missing, fragile or failing silently — the clearest way to see how Pakvaro works before anything larger. Agencies can also engage Pakvaro for partner delivery under their own brand when their team is at capacity.

Proof first. Release on pass. See where your workflow can fail before you hire anyone, approve the test before work starts, and pay into Upwork escrow — we ask you to release it only after you have watched that test pass.

What a Workflow Sprint includes

  • One workflow in n8n, Make or Zapier
  • Up to three connected systems
  • One agreed trigger, one defined output and one error-alert path
  • A recorded acceptance test using an agreed test case
  • A runbook covering trigger, mapping, credentials, the error path and safe reruns
  • Seven calendar days of fixes for defects against the agreed acceptance test
  • Delivered within 72 business hours once scope, access and test data are ready

FAQ

Before we talk.

If the scope fits, a short intro call confirms the workflow and the first acceptance test.

How long does an engagement take?

A single-workflow Sprint is delivered within 72 business hours once the written scope, least-privilege access, representative test data and a named approver are in place. Client-side delays, platform outages and unavailable third-party APIs pause the clock.

What counts as one workflow?

One agreed trigger, one defined output and one error-alert path across no more than three systems. A new system, field, branch or business rule is handled through written change control.

What is not included?

Production data migrations; regulated, medical or financial decisions; private-data scraping; 24/7 support; unlimited changes; unsupported APIs; and more than one workflow or three systems.

Where is the workflow built?

In accounts owned and paid for by you. Access should be least-privilege and removable after handover. Third-party subscriptions and usage charges remain yours.

What happens if the agreed test fails?

Pakvaro gets one cure window of up to five business days for a Pakvaro-caused in-scope failure. If the same check still fails, you may cancel and receive the full milestone back.

Can I send real data or credentials by email?

No. Start with synthetic or redacted samples. Do not send passwords, API keys, production exports or confidential code through ordinary email.

Do you work on IoT or messaging workflows?

Yes, each within a separate fixed scope: IoT data platforms such as ThingsBoard (see the Device-to-Demo Sprint), and messaging workflows on the WhatsApp Business Platform (see WhatsApp lead response, India).
