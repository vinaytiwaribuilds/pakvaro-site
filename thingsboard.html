FAQ

Questions teams ask first.

If the scope fits, a short intro call confirms it and the written checks follow.

Is my data safe?

Work happens in your own ThingsBoard tenant, licence or Cloud account using time-limited, least-privilege access where practical. Customer personal data, credentials, keys, production exports and confidential code are handled only through the agreed project systems.

Which platform do you use?

ThingsBoard, in the account and licence you control. Pakvaro is not affiliated with or endorsed by ThingsBoard, and does not provide licence advice.

Who owns the work?

You keep your account, data, keys, billing and the handed-over configuration, evidence and runbook. The signed statement of work records the exact access and deliverables.

What do you need from us?

One working MQTT, HTTP or LoRaWAN payload; field names, units and timezone; a sandbox account; alarm values; the report rule; and one named person for decisions and acceptance.

How long does it take?

Delivery is within 15 business days after complete inputs and access, with a 10-business-day target. The written start date is confirmed after the inputs are complete.

How do I pay?

India: 100% in advance by bank transfer or UPI, with an invoice. Global: 100% in advance, or a funded Upwork milestone. Platform or subscription costs are paid directly by you.

Do you do on-site work?

No. Delivery is remote; your installer commissions devices, connectivity and field hardware.
