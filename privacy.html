1. Services available today

Pakvaro.com

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Pakvaro does not add analytics, advertising pixels or cookies to it. GitHub may log ordinary service and website usage information such as an IP address, device or browser information, request date and time, referring page, pages viewed and links clicked under GitHub's privacy statement. Pakvaro does not receive a visitor-level analytics dashboard from this site.

Email

If you email hello@pakvaro.com, Pakvaro receives the information you send, your name and email address, message history and ordinary email metadata. Zoho Mail provides the mailbox in its India data centre. Pakvaro uses the message to answer you, take requested pre-contract steps, keep a business-conversation record and, if necessary, protect legal rights.

Booking

The booking link opens Cal.com. Cal.com may collect booking details and technical information under its own privacy notice. Do not put credentials, private telemetry or sensitive information in booking fields. Cal.com's processing location applicable on this notice's effective date has not yet been verified by Pakvaro.

Public Device Readiness Check

The free check at app.pakvaro.com/readiness runs deterministic checks entirely in your browser. The sample you paste is not sent to or stored by Pakvaro. The public check has no account, and Pakvaro sets no cookies on it. Its hosting provider may still process ordinary request and security logs. Use only synthetic or redacted telemetry; never paste credentials, personal data, certificates, firmware or production exports.

2. Business outreach

Pakvaro may keep a business contact's name, role, company, work email or published business contact channel, company website, country, the public company-controlled page where the details appeared, message history, project-fit notes and opt-out status. Details come from the person, an introduction, or a public company website, contact page, team page or professional company profile. Pakvaro does not use bought lists, private contact details or information about private life.

The information is used to assess whether the Device-to-Demo Sprint may fit, send one relevant first message and no more than one follow-up, and keep a record of the conversation. It is not sold, rented, added to a newsletter or used for automated profiling. Where the GDPR applies, proportionate business outreach relies on legitimate interests subject to a balancing check and any stricter local electronic-marketing rule. Enquiries are handled to respond to the request or take requested pre-contract steps.

If no project starts, ordinary enquiry and outreach records are deleted or anonymised 12 months after the last interaction. After an opt-out, Pakvaro keeps only the minimum suppression record needed to prevent another message. Reply “stop” to opt out; Pakvaro honours it the same day.

3. Pakvaro Workspace

This section applies when Pakvaro Workspace opens for sign-ups. Workspace accounts are for business users aged 18 or older.

Information and purposes

  • Account information: name, business email, password handled by Supabase Auth, title, time zone, role, account status and sign-in information. This is used to create and operate the account, authenticate the user and support the service. The basis is the Workspace contract and requested pre-contract steps.
  • Organisation and billing information: organisation name, country, website, members and invitations, business contact and billing details, payment route, invoices and related records. This is used to set up the organisation, administer a Sprint, invoice and keep business records. The basis is contract and applicable record-keeping duties.
  • Device and readiness information: protocol, redacted or synthetic payload samples, device context, declared scope, saved readiness reports, findings and project inputs. This is used to run requested checks, save reports and scope or deliver a Sprint. The basis is contract or requested pre-contract steps.
  • Project files: files and evidence that a user or Pakvaro uploads, together with file name, type, size, project, uploader and time. This is used to exchange authorised project material and deliver the Sprint. The basis is contract.
  • Messages and decisions: project messages, revisions, change requests, decisions and related timestamps. This is used for project communication, change control and business evidence. The basis is contract and the need to establish or defend legal rights.
  • Acceptance and consent metadata: proposals and delivery acceptances, failed-check reports, document versions, acknowledgement or consent value, time and user agent. This is used to prove terms acceptance, operate the consent gate and keep contract evidence. Required account processing relies on contract; consent is used only for the optional readiness follow-up email.
  • Security and service records: IP address and request, authentication, abuse-prevention, error and audit information created by Pakvaro's providers or the Workspace. This is used to authenticate users, prevent abuse, diagnose faults and maintain tamper-evident business records. The basis is contract and legitimate interests in operating and protecting the service.

The optional readiness follow-up choice is off by default. If enabled, Pakvaro may send no more than one message and one follow-up about a saved readiness report. It can be withdrawn in Workspace notification settings or by replying “stop”, without affecting account access.

Providers and locations

  • Supabase Inc. on AWS South Asia (Mumbai), ap-south-1: Workspace database, authentication and file storage. The selected specific region stores the primary project data in Mumbai; a region choice is not a compliance certification. Pakvaro must confirm the production project's selected region at go-live.
  • Cloudflare: Cloudflare Pages hosts the static Workspace application, and Turnstile performs a bot check on authentication forms. Turnstile processes browser-environment and request signals needed for the check, not the form entries. Static application assets contain no customer project data. Cloudflare processing and log locations for Pakvaro's selected Free configuration have not yet been verified.
  • Resend: sends Workspace transactional email from go-live and receives names, email addresses, message content and delivery logs. Resend says this data is stored in the United States; on its Free, Pro and Scale plans, email and log data is retained for 30 days, backups for 7 days, and remaining customer data is deleted within 90 days after account termination. Pakvaro's sending region has not yet been verified.
  • Cal.com: handles call booking when a user chooses the booking link. Its own privacy notice applies. The processing location applicable on this notice's effective date has not yet been verified by Pakvaro.

Pakvaro authorises a new Workspace provider that handles customer data only through the signed SOW or change-control process where required.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Account data is kept while the account is active, until deletion and for no more than 30 days afterward. Pakvaro processes a deletion request within 30 days.
  • Deletion anonymises the person and removes memberships. The account then appears as a deleted or former user, while necessary acceptance, invoice, change-order and audit evidence is retained.
  • Raw readiness samples saved in Workspace are kept for 90 days.
  • Project files are purged 30 days after a project is marked closed, unless a dispute is open. Pakvaro records the purge and sends any confirmation required by the SOW.
  • Audit events are kept for at least one year.
  • Acceptances, invoices and change orders are kept for the statutory contract and tax evidence period. The exact period in years has not yet been confirmed; Pakvaro will set it after professional tax advice and before outside Workspace sign-ups.

Provider copies may follow the provider-specific periods described above. Legal, tax, dispute or security records may be kept longer where the law or an active dispute requires it.

5. Security

Workspace uses HTTPS in transit. Its primary database and files are configured for Supabase's Mumbai region. Access rules separate organisations; project file links expire after 60 seconds; administrative access requires multi-factor authentication; and the audit log is designed to be tamper-evident. No internet service is risk-free, and these controls are not a certification.

6. Your rights and complaints

You may ask Pakvaro to give you access to your personal data, correct it, erase it where applicable, provide an export, explain the processing and providers, or record a nomination for another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity. Use Settings > Data in Workspace or email hello@pakvaro.com. Pakvaro may verify your identity and will respond within 30 days.

International users may also request GDPR-style access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability rights where relevant. Pakvaro honours these requests as a matter of practice but does not claim to have an EU representative.

Grievance Officer: Vinay Tiwari, hello@pakvaro.com. Complaints under the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 will be resolved within one month. Where applicable, you also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India after using Pakvaro's grievance route.

7. Sharing and required disclosure

Pakvaro uses information only for the purposes above and shares it only with the relevant providers, authorised professional advisers, an authorised Sprint partner named in the SOW, or a public authority where legally required. Pakvaro does not sell or rent personal data.

8. Changes to this notice

Pakvaro will publish a revised notice with a new version id and effective date. If a change affects Workspace processing or required acknowledgements, Workspace users will be sent to /consent to acknowledge the current versions before using other Workspace routes.
