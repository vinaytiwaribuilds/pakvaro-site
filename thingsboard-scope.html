Device-to-Demo Sprint

One-page scope22 October 2026 · Rev B

Outcome

One existing MQTT, HTTP or LoRaWAN payload becomes a customer-ready ThingsBoard demonstration in the customer's own tenant, licence or Cloud account.

Good fit

A working device, stable payload, named decision-maker, real demo or pilot date, non-regulated data and remote delivery.

Included

  • Customer logins and tenant isolation
  • One live dashboard
  • One offline alarm and one threshold alarm
  • One sample weekly report (PDF/CSV)
  • Applicable written acceptance checks
  • Recorded handover and configuration export

Not included

Field work or installation, hardware, firmware changes, native apps, regulated or medical data, life-safety or remote-actuation work, 24/7 support, production hosting or cutover, licence or legal advice.

Inputs required

One sample payload; field names, types, units and timezone; a sandbox account; offline and threshold values; the report rule; a named acceptance representative; and written authority to access the account.

Acceptance and handover

Checks are agreed before work. A peer reviewer (personal acquaintance) re-runs them. The customer then runs the applicable checks and receives the configuration, evidence, runbook and recorded handover.

Price

USD 500 (global)
₹14,999 total; no GST is charged (India)

Payment

India: 100% in advance by bank transfer or UPI, with an invoice. Global: 100% in advance, or a funded Upwork milestone.

Delivery

Within 15 business days after complete inputs and access, with a 10-business-day target.

Cure and delay

If a Pakvaro-caused check fails, Pakvaro fixes it within 5 business days. If it still fails, Pakvaro refunds the unearned amount. One cure window only. If a Pakvaro-caused delay passes the written date by more than 5 business days, the customer may cancel and receive a refund of the unearned amount.

Start date and reference price

Next start: 2 Nov 2026 (India and global).
