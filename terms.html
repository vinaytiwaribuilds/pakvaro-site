A paid Sprint is governed by the signed Statement of Work and Master Services Agreement. Those signed documents prevail over these terms for the paid engagement. No message or Workspace status changes the signed scope, price, date, acceptance criterion, data use or subprocessor; those changes require the signed change-control process.

Price and payment

The standard India price is ₹14,999 total. No GST is charged (Pakvaro is not GST-registered). The standard global price is USD 500. Payment is 100% in advance (bank transfer / UPI, with an invoice), or, for a global Upwork engagement, a funded Upwork milestone. Work does not start until payment clears or the milestone shows as funded and the signed SOW's complete inputs and access are available.

Pakvaro will never change bank details by message. If any payment instruction appears to change, do not pay until the details have been confirmed with Vinay Tiwari on a call arranged through a known Pakvaro channel.

Acceptance, one cure and refund

The customer runs the applicable objective checks in the signed SOW and either accepts the delivery or gives Pakvaro written, reproducible evidence of a Pakvaro-caused failure of an in-scope check. New preferences, changed inputs and out-of-scope work are not acceptance defects.

Pakvaro has one 5-business-day cure window and fixes and resubmits a Pakvaro-caused failed check within 5 business days. If that check still fails, Pakvaro refunds the unearned amount. One cure window only. There is no second cure window unless the parties sign a change order. The unearned amount is the portion of the selected SOW fee attributable to in-scope work not completed and accepted; Pakvaro provides a written completion-and-refund calculation.

For this purpose, a business day means a day other than Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday in Indore, Madhya Pradesh.
