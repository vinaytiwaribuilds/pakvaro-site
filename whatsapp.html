Scope

The complete first-response handoff, in one setup.

Included

  • One website form or one Meta lead form
  • One official WhatsApp Business Platform number
  • One acknowledgement and up to five agreed qualification questions
  • One owner or team alert route
  • One Google Sheet or one supported CRM destination
  • One daily missed-lead report
  • Human handoff rules, synthetic test enquiries, a recorded acceptance test and a short runbook
  • Business-hours support during setup and seven calendar days of defect fixes

Recorded acceptance

A test lead must make the whole trip.

Using test data and a test or approved number, the recorded check covers:

  1. 01

    Consent and response

    A consented test lead enters from the agreed source and the approved acknowledgement attempt is logged.

  2. 02

    Qualification

    All agreed fields are captured, or an incomplete lead is visibly flagged.

  3. 03

    Alert and record

    The owner alert arrives and one correctly mapped Sheet or CRM record is created without an unintended duplicate.

  4. 04

    Human handoff

    The lead reaches the agreed handoff state and an unresolved test lead appears in the missed-lead report.

Consent and account ownership

Official, client-owned and permission-based.

01

Official platform only

No unofficial WhatsApp tools, browser automation, bought lists, bulk cold messages or Pakvaro-owned shared accounts.

02

You keep control

You own the Meta Business Portfolio, phone number, templates, lead data and billing. Do not send passwords, OTPs or permanent tokens by email or chat.

03

Consent is required

You provide a clear opt-in, privacy notice and opt-out handling. Work may pause if consent, account authority or permitted use cannot be shown.

●
